# ChargeGuard privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

ChargeGuard is a Wear OS app that warns you in the evening if your watch battery is too low to last the night.

## What the app collects
Nothing. ChargeGuard does not collect, store or send any personal information. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What the app does on your watch
- It reads your watch's own battery level, on the watch, to decide whether to alert you. The battery level never leaves the watch.
- It stores a few settings (such as the alert level you choose) on the watch itself.

## The one network request
To let the developer change the alert time and wording without a new install, the app downloads a small public settings file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`). The app sends no information about you or your watch in that request. As with any website request, GitHub can see the internet address the request came from; that is governed by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Permissions
- Exact alarms: to run the daily check at the chosen evening time.
- Full-screen alert and display over other apps: to show the flashing alert when the battery is low.
- Vibration: to buzz with the alert.
- Internet: to download the settings file described above.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
